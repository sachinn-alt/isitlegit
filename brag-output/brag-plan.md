# Brag Plan: IsItLegit

## 1. Project Planning Rubric

1. **What is the app?**
   IsItLegit is a client-side cybersecurity intelligence engine and PWA that differentiates genuine transactional alerts and bank OTPs from weaponized phishing scams and evasion loopholes.

2. **What is the funniest or most impressive claim?**
   "Dissect weaponized links, fake bank SMS alerts, and homoglyph lookalikes. 100% private — zero info is ever stored; everything vanishes immediately after check."

3. **What is the visual hook?**
   Stark Bauhaus constructivist visual system: primary triad (#D02020 Red, #1040C0 Blue, #F0C020 Yellow), solid #121212 borders, 8px hard offset shadows, zero border-radius, and animated threat meter gauges.

4. **What should be shown from the actual UI?**
   - Hero: `INSPECT BEFORE YOU CLICK.`
   - Scanner Console with multi-input tabs (Link, Message, Image/QR, Breach Audit)
   - Real-time RFC 3986 loophole defense (@ authority camouflage, DWORD IP conversion)
   - Algorithmic Verification Matrix & Circular Threat Score Dossier (92 Threat vs 98 Genuine)
   - Pre-Click Phone Screen Guardian gate

5. **What is the shortest satisfying video?**
   18.5 seconds (tight 5-scene cinematic arc).

6. **What tone fits best?**
   - **Preset**: `cinematic` / `polished`
   - **Creative Direction**: High-contrast Bauhaus constructivist cybersecurity trailer. Sharp typography, mechanical cuts, punchy reveals.

7. **What should the audio feel like?**
   Modern driving electronic pulse with tactile click/scanner impacts and a clean final resolution.
   - **Track**: `assets/music/happy-beats-business-moves-vol-10-by-ende-dot-app.mp3`

8. **What should the share caption say?**
   "Built IsItLegit: an on-device, zero-retention cybersecurity engine with client-side ML and RFC 3986 network loophole defense. Inspect before you click. 🛡️"

9. **What's the user flow worth showing?**
   - Beat 1: Enter link / trigger deceptive URL (`https://paypal.com@evil-trap.xyz`)
   - Beat 2: Algorithmic de-cloaking (traps @ authority camouflage & calculates P = 92% threat)
   - Beat 3: Definitive Diagnostic Dossier & Phone Screen Guardian blocking attack
